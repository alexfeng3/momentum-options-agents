# Social posts — Build in Public

Drafts for the two required posts. Both must be **posted from your own
accounts** — I can't post on your behalf.

| File | Platform | Note |
|---|---|---|
| `x_post.md` | X / Twitter | Two length variants — pick based on whether your account has extended post length |
| `linkedin_post.md` | LinkedIn | Single version, well under the 3000-char limit |

## Tagging — do this in the actual composer, not by pasting plain text

**X:** typing `@lablabai` and `@AlpacaHQ` directly in the post auto-links them as
you type — already in both drafts.

**LinkedIn:** typing `@` and then `lablab.ai` or `Alpaca` should surface each
company page in the mention dropdown — click it to turn it into a real,
notifying mention. A plain-text "lablab.ai" or "Alpaca" in the draft will
**not** notify or link them; you have to select it from the dropdown when you
post. If either doesn't resolve, the target pages are:
- lablab.ai: https://www.linkedin.com/company/lablab-ai/
- Alpaca: https://www.linkedin.com/company/9300556/

## After posting

The hackathon accepts up to 5 post links with your submission — these two
count as a start. Save each post's URL once it's live; you'll need them for
the submission form.
